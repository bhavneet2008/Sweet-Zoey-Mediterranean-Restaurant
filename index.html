Our Story

A Family-Owned
Mediterranean Gem

Nestled in the heart of Casper, Wyoming, Sweet Zoey is more than a restaurant — it's a gathering place where generations of Mediterranean tradition come alive on every plate.

We're known for our homemade baklava, fresh gyros, perfectly grilled kabobs, silky hummus, and signature bubble teas. Every dish is made from scratch daily using time-honored family recipes, served by a team that treats you like family.

Walk in and feel the warmth — the aroma of fresh spices, the sizzle of the grill, and the genuine smiles that greet you at the door. This is what authentic Mediterranean hospitality looks like.
